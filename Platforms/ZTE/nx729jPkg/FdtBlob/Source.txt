Compiled from the Kettle tree's kernel/dts/qcom/sm8550-nx729j.dts (mainline 7.3.3, arm64).

Why this DTB is in the firmware: DisplayDxe is a Qualcomm DXE driver that locates MDSS and the
DSI controllers by walking a device tree. Mu's nx729j ACPI tables describe no display hardware
at all, so without this blob DisplayDxe fails to map the MDSS base address, drops to SWRender
mode, and the panel never lights. DtPlatformDxe publishes this blob to it.

The panel itself is not described here for DisplayDxe's benefit: it detects the panel over DSI
and matches one of the vendor XMLs already embedded from Binaries/nx729j/RawFiles (including
Panel_zte_bf068_rm692h0_6p97_magic_dsc_cmd.xml, which is this device's panel). All this blob
has to provide is display-subsystem@ae00000 (qcom,sm8550-mdss) and dsi@ae94000/ae96000
(qcom,mdss-dsi-ctrl), the same arrangement pongPkg uses.

Keep it small: FD_SIZE is 0x300000 and the FD is already full.
